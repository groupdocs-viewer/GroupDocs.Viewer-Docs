Supply agreement - draft 2 for review
Between Harbor Lane Provisions (the Buyer) and Northwind Coffee Roasters (the Supplier).
1. Products and prices
The Supplier delivers whole bean coffee at the prices in Schedule A, fixed for twelve months.
2. Delivery
Deliveries arrive at the Buyer's North Pier site within five working days of each order.
3. Payment
The Buyer pays each invoice within 45 days. Late payments carry interest at 1 percent a month.
4. Term
The agreement starts on 1 March 2026 and runs for two years, then renews yearly unless either party gives three months' notice.
