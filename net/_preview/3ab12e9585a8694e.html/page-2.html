A letter from our Managing Director  
Dear colleagues, customers and partners,  
2025 was the best year in our history. Net revenue grew 15 percent to $21.4 million, we shipped 55,300  
orders, and 1,265 cafes, grocers and restaurants now buy from us every month. On-time delivery reached  
97.4 percent, even through the busiest holiday season we have had.  
“We grew because our customers trust us to deliver good food  
on time, every time.”  
Growth has a cost: our Quayside warehouse ran at 94 percent of capacity from September. In December the  
board approved the North Pier distribution centre, a second site with 6,500 pallet positions and a cold chain  
for cheese and dairy. It opens in late 2026, and this report explains how we will pay for it.  
You can follow the project on our website.  
Thank you for an extraordinary year.  
Grace Holloway  
Managing Director
