3. Safety on the floor
Forklifts and pallet trucks
Only staff with a valid licence drive forklifts. Walk in the marked aisles, wear your high-visibility vest and safety shoes, and never walk under a raised load.
Racking
Report any damaged upright or beam at once: the bay is closed until it is checked. Never climb the racking.
Reporting an accident
Tell your team leader about every accident or near miss, however small, and record it in the accident book in the staff room before the end of your shift.
4. The cold rooms
Temperatures
The chilled rooms hold 2 to 5 degrees Celsius. If a display reads outside that range, close the door, call your team leader and do not move any stock.
Clothing and time limits
Wear the insulated jacket and gloves from the rack by the door, and work no more than 45 minutes at a time in a chilled room before a break in the warm.
