5. Goods in and goods out
Booking a dock
Suppliers book a dock at least 48 hours ahead through ruth.okafor@harborlane.example. Unbooked lorries wait until a dock is free.
Checking a delivery
Count every case against the delivery note, check the dates and the temperature of chilled goods, and sign only for what arrived.
Picking and packing
Pick in the order the handheld shows, scan every case, and seal and label each pallet before it leaves the packing area. Orders placed by 2 pm leave the same day.
