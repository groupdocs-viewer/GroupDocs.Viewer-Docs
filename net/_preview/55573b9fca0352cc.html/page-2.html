1. Welcome to North Pier
The site
The North Pier distribution centre at 2 North Pier Road, Port Ellery opens on 1 December 2026. It holds 6,500 pallet positions, of which 1,300 are chilled, and ships to every customer north of the harbour.
Who is who
Ruth Okafor runs the site. Jonas Weber is responsible for transport and the warehouse system, and each shift has a team leader, named on the board by the staff entrance.
2. Your shifts
Shift times
The early shift works from 6:00 to 14:00 and the late shift from 14:00 to 22:00, Monday to Saturday. A night shift starts in February 2027.
Breaks and overtime
Every shift has a 30-minute break and two 10-minute breaks. Overtime is agreed with your team leader before the shift ends and is paid at time and a half.
