4. Funding
Amara Osei proposed paying for the budget with a seven-year term loan of $3.5 million from Port Ellery Savings Bank at a fixed 5.9 percent, and $2.17 million from cash, which stood at $4.8 million at the end of November. The loan keeps net debt below one and a half times earnings throughout.
5. Decisions
1.The board approved the North Pier budget of $5,670,000, contingency included.
2.The board approved the term loan of $3,500,000 and authorised the Managing Director and the Finance Director to sign it.
3.Spending beyond the contingency needs the board's approval in advance.
4.The North Pier distribution centre opens on 1 December 2026.
