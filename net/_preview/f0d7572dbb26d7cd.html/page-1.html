Board minutes: North Pier budget
Meeting of the board of Harbor Lane Provisions, held at 18 Quayside Road, Port Ellery on Monday 8 December 2025 at 9:00.
Present: Grace Holloway (Managing Director, chair), Amara Osei (Finance Director), Jonas Weber (Operations Manager), Mateo Silva (Sales Lead).
In attendance: Ruth Okafor (North Pier project lead) for item 3; Lena Park (Procurement Lead), minutes.
1. Minutes of the last meeting
The minutes of the meeting of 10 November 2025 were approved as a true record. There were no matters arising.
2. Trading update
Amara Osei reported net revenue of $19.6 million for the eleven months to November, 15 percent up on the same months of 2024, and a gross margin of 32.8 percent. The Quayside warehouse has run above 90 percent of its capacity since September, and the holiday peak will take it to its limit.
3. North Pier budget
Ruth Okafor presented the budget for the North Pier distribution centre at 2 North Pier Road, Port Ellery: 6,500 pallet positions, a cold chain for cheese and dairy, 18 dock doors and a new warehouse management system, ready for the first shipments on 1 December 2026.
