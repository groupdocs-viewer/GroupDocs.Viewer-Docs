A letter from our Managing Director
Dear colleagues, customers and partners,
2025 was the best year in our history. Net revenue grew 15 percent to $21.4 million, we shipped 55,300 orders, and 1,265 cafes, grocers and restaurants now buy from us every month. On-time delivery reached 97.4 percent, even through the busiest holiday season we have had.
“We grew because our customers trust us to deliver good food on time, every time.”
Growth has a cost: our Quayside warehouse ran at 94 percent of capacity from September. In December the board approved the North Pier distribution centre, a second site with 6,500 pallet positions and a cold chain for cheese and dairy. It opens in late 2026, and this report explains how we will pay for it.

Thank you for an extraordinary year.

What 2026 brings
Three things matter this year: opening the North Pier distribution centre on time and on budget, moving every customer to online re-ordering, and keeping delivery above 97 percent on time while volumes grow. Each has one owner on the management team, and we will report on all three every quarter.
