# Harbor Lane Provisions - wholesale terms of service

Effective 1 October 2026. Version 4.2.

**In short:** order by 2 pm for next-day delivery, pay within 30 days, and tell us within 48 hours if anything is wrong.

## 1. About these terms

These terms apply to every order a business customer places with Harbor Lane Provisions
("Harbor Lane", "we", "us"), online, by e-mail, by phone or through an account manager. By
placing an order you accept them.

If you have a separate written agreement with us, that agreement wins where the two
disagree.

Summary of section 1:

1. These terms apply to every order a business customer places with Harbor Lane Provisions ("Harbor Lane", "we", "us"), online, by e-mail, by phone or through an account manager.
2. If you have a separate written agreement with us, that agreement wins where the two disagree.

## 2. Your account

You need a wholesale account to order. We open accounts for cafes, restaurants, grocers,
hotels and other food businesses.

Keep your account details up to date, especially your delivery address and the people
allowed to order.

- **Credit limit:** set when we open your account and reviewed every year.
- **Users:** up to five people per account can order online.

Summary of section 2:

1. You need a wholesale account to order.
2. Keep your account details up to date, especially your delivery address and the people allowed to order.

## 3. Orders

An order is confirmed when we send you an order confirmation. We reserve the stock for you
from that moment.

You can change or cancel an order until **2 pm on the working day before delivery**. After
that we may charge for goods we have already picked.

If an item is out of stock we tell you before delivery and suggest a replacement; we never
substitute without asking.

- Minimum order: $150 before tax.
- Seasonal ranges, such as Autumn Harvest, are available while stocks last.

Summary of section 3:

1. An order is confirmed when we send you an order confirmation.
2. You can change or cancel an order until **2 pm on the working day before delivery**.
3. If an item is out of stock we tell you before delivery and suggest a replacement; we never substitute without asking.

## 4. Prices and payment

Prices are in US dollars, per unit, delivered, and exclude sales tax. Our price list
changes on 1 January and 1 July; we give you 30 days' notice of any other change.

We invoice each delivery. Pay within **30 days** of the invoice date by bank transfer or
card. Early payment within 10 days earns a 2 percent discount.

We may charge interest of 1 percent a month on overdue invoices and pause deliveries to
accounts more than 15 days overdue.

Summary of section 4:

1. Prices are in US dollars, per unit, delivered, and exclude sales tax.
2. We invoice each delivery.
3. We may charge interest of 1 percent a month on overdue invoices and pause deliveries to accounts more than 15 days overdue.

## 5. Delivery

We deliver Monday to Saturday from our Quayside warehouse and, from 1 December 2026, from
the North Pier distribution centre.

Orders placed by 2 pm are delivered the next working day. We agree a delivery window with
you and tell you if we will miss it.

Chilled and frozen goods travel in temperature-controlled vans and must be received by a
member of your staff, who signs for them.

- **Delivery charge:** free over $300, otherwise $18.
- **Access:** tell us about steps, narrow doors or delivery time limits.

Summary of section 5:

1. We deliver Monday to Saturday from our Quayside warehouse and, from 1 December 2026, from the North Pier distribution centre.
2. Orders placed by 2 pm are delivered the next working day.
3. Chilled and frozen goods travel in temperature-controlled vans and must be received by a member of your staff, who signs for them.

## 6. Returns and credits

Check every delivery. Report missing, damaged or short-dated items within **48 hours** and
we credit or replace them.

We do not take back goods you ordered by mistake once they are delivered, unless we agree
otherwise.

Summary of section 6:

1. Check every delivery.
2. We do not take back goods you ordered by mistake once they are delivered, unless we agree otherwise.
